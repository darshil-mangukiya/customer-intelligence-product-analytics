# Retention Lifecycle Report

- Second-purchase conversion by day 60: 18.3%
- Customers still waiting for second purchase at day 60: 81.7%
- Largest first-60-day path: Paid Search / Apparel

## Lifecycle Stage Summary
| Stage | Recommended Transition | Customers | Avg CLV | Avg Recency | Avg Return Rate |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Dormant or lost | Suppress broad discounting; test low-cost reactivation | 160,806 | $26 | 824 | 6.1% |
| Active repeat | Nurture to next category | 30,697 | $138 | 57 | 17.1% |
| At risk | Win back with margin-aware offer | 25,996 | $155 | 178 | 15.7% |
| High-value loyal | Protect value with loyalty benefits | 20,048 | $955 | 50 | 14.1% |
| Warming risk | Re-engage before 120-day inactivity | 12,453 | $154 | 91 | 15.8% |

## Business Use
- Treat the first 60 days as the activation window for repeat purchase conversion.
- Separate warming-risk customers from dormant customers so retention spend is not applied too late.
- Track lifecycle stage mix as an operating KPI alongside churn, retention, and CLV.
