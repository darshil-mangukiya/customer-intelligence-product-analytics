# Customer Intelligence Business Requirements

These requirements define a reference workflow for retention, customer strategy, Finance, Product, analytics, and BI roles. They were tested against the repository's generated data and automated UAT evidence.

| ID | Persona | Requirement | Acceptance summary |
|---|---|---|---|
| CR-001 | Retention Manager | Identify high-value segments with elevated governed churn risk. | Segment, risk tier, count, CLV, exposure, period, and source are present. |
| CR-002 | Data Analyst | Identify statistically supported factors associated with churn. | Driver method, strength, p-value, interpretation, and non-causal limitation are traceable. |
| CR-003 | Finance Business Partner | Estimate CLV and revenue exposure for high-risk populations. | Historical inputs and scenario estimates are separately labeled and reconciled. |
| CR-004 | Product Manager | Distinguish statistical significance from practical significance. | Readout contains both conclusions, threshold, CI, p-value, and effect size. |
| CR-005 | Customer Strategy Manager | Identify valuable segments whose RFM position deteriorated. | Prior/current segment, transition, count, value, and risk changes are available. |
| CR-006 | Data Analyst | Validate experiment allocation with SRM before interpretation. | Expected/actual allocation, chi-square, p-value, alpha, status, and warning are output. |
| CR-007 | Product Manager | Review a registered experiment's question, hypothesis, population, metrics, design, and decision. | Registry, deterministic readout, and decision log share the experiment ID. |
| CR-008 | Finance Business Partner | Compare five retention scenarios without presenting estimates as observed impact. | Assumptions, costs, preserved value, net benefit, ROI, and limitations are explicit. |
| CR-009 | Retention Manager | Prioritize groups for review using governed churn, CLV, migration, driver, and experiment evidence. | Action center has evidence, priority, recommendation, and `NEEDS_REVIEW`. |
| CR-010 | BI Developer | Consume machine-readable migration, economics, action, reconciliation, and alert outputs. | CSV schemas are stable and generated by one workflow. |
| CR-011 | Data Analyst | Reconcile Python and base-R experiment statistics within defined tolerances. | Automated reconciliation reports every metric and overall PASS/FAIL. |
| CR-012 | Customer Success Manager | Review recommendations without triggering communication or system writes. | Outputs are advisory and expose no execution action. |
| CR-013 | BI Developer | Reconcile customer, churn, CLV, experiment, migration, and action-center populations. | Automated checks have source/output values, tolerance, and status. |
| CR-014 | Customer Strategy Manager | Receive explainable alerts for material shifts or invalidity. | Alert type, value, threshold, status, and interpretation are present. |
| CR-015 | Data Analyst | Produce a deterministic aggregate insight packet as the AI evidence source. | Required sections exist; JSON contains no NaN/Infinity or customer identifiers. |
| CR-016 | Customer Strategy Manager | Ask governed questions through one advisory copilot. | Structured response uses approved evidence and surfaces assumptions, uncertainty, and review needs. |
| CR-017 | Data Analyst | Operate the AI feature locally without external credentials. | Fake and disabled providers work; OpenAI requires environment variables. |
| CR-018 | BI Developer | Refresh the customer-intelligence lifecycle with one command and audit its stages. | `make customer-intelligence` writes a stage manifest and generated evidence. |

## Business rules

- No protected or sensitive characteristic is used as a decision rule.
- Churn and CLV are modeled estimates; observational drivers are associations, not causes.
- Scenario outputs retain their assumptions and estimate labels.
- Failed SRM blocks reliable experiment interpretation.
- Recommendations remain `NEEDS_REVIEW`; no campaign, CRM, pricing, or allocation update is executed.
- Every public claim must remain traceable to deterministic synthetic-data outputs.
