# Product Analytics Methodology

Product intelligence methodology.

## Profitability

- Use return-adjusted profit and margin, not gross revenue only.

## Retention Impact

- Compare products and categories by repeat customer rate and cohort retention.

## Risk Association

- Identify products linked to returns, churn risk, discount dependency, or margin leakage.

## Affinity

- Use confidence, lift, and affinity score for cross-sell and bundle opportunities.
