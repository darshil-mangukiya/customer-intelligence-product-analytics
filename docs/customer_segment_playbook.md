# Customer Segment Playbook

Recommended actions by customer segment.

## High Value Loyal Customers

- Protect with VIP service, early access, and margin-aware loyalty benefits.

## Discount-Driven Buyers

- Use controlled incentives, bundles, and margin-safe promotions.

## At-Risk Customers

- Prioritize based on expected profit at risk and CLV band.

## One-Time Buyers

- Drive second purchase with category-specific onboarding.
