# Churn Intervention Playbook

Business playbook for using churn outputs.

## Critical Risk

- Launch save journey when CLV/profit at risk is high.
- Use service recovery when return or support friction is elevated.

## High Discount Dependency

- Use margin-controlled offers and replenishment messaging rather than blanket discounts.

## One-Time Buyers

- Send first-to-second purchase journey within first 30 to 60 days.

## Product Fit Risk

- Route return-heavy product cohorts to merchandising review.
