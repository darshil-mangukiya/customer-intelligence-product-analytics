# Cohort Methodology

Cohort and retention methodology.

## Cohorts

- Monthly acquisition cohorts using first purchase month.

## Retention

- Month 1 through Month 12 customer retention, revenue retention, repeat purchase retention, and behavioral retention.

## Slices

- Analyze by acquisition channel, first product category, region, and segment.

## Health Score

- Cohort health combines retention, revenue retention, repeat behavior, and churn exposure.
