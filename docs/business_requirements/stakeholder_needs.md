# Stakeholder Needs

Reference needs used to define the platform's requirements and acceptance criteria.

## Executive Sponsor

- Needs one view of customer growth, churn risk, revenue leakage, and profitability.
- Wants retention recommendations tied to value, not just customer count.
- Needs confidence that the data is validated before leadership meetings.

## Lifecycle Marketing

- Needs an exportable list of customers by action priority.
- Wants to know which segments respond to offers.
- Needs holdout logic so campaigns can be measured incrementally.

## Product / Merchandising

- Needs visibility into products that drive returns, low margin, or poor retention.
- Wants cross-sell ideas connected to product affinity and profitability.
- Needs category-level performance beyond top-line sales.

## BI / Analytics Engineering

- Needs stable marts, documented grains, semantic KPI definitions, and data contracts.
- Wants refresh run status, validation results, and monitoring watch items.
- Needs Power BI/Tableau build guidance and release QA checklists.
